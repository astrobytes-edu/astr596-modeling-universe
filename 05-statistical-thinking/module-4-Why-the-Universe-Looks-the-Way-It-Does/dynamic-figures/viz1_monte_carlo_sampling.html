Key Insights for Students:

  • Inverse Transform Method: Uniform random numbers ξ ∈ [0,1] become exponentially distributed τ = -ln(ξ)
  • Physical Meaning: Most interactions occur at small τ (near surfaces), few at large τ (deep in medium)
  • Validation: Histogram should match theoretical curve p(τ) = e-τ
  • Implementation: This is the core of every Monte Carlo radiative transfer code

Pedagogical Value:

Why This Visualization Matters: Students often struggle to connect abstract mathematical expressions like τ = -ln(ξ) to actual code implementation. This interactive demonstration shows the statistical mechanics in action, building intuition for how Monte Carlo methods work. The ability to adjust parameters helps students understand how sample size affects convergence to the theoretical distribution.
